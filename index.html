Aliens Land in School Parking Lot

Eye Witness Accounts

“I was walking toward the school when I heard this huge humming sound,” said sophomore Jake Miller, an eyewitness to the landing. “I looked up and saw this shiny silver ship coming down into the parking lot. Everyone started screaming and taking out their phones, but the aliens didn’t seem dangerous at all. They just looked around, waved at us, and walked toward the school like they were visiting for the day. It was probably the strangest thing I’ve ever seen.”

Official Government Statement

The Department of Public Safety released a statement Tuesday confirming that the spacecraft landing at Willow Creek High School did not pose an immediate threat to students, staff, or the surrounding community.
